# Behavioral Compilation

From Harness to Weights on a Single Machine

A governed study of narrow LLM specialization on Apple Silicon,
inspired by production-scale agent learning.

Philippe Dagher  
SENONI Research  
Working Paper 2026-02 · 13 September 2026

[Download the PDF](Behavioral-Compilation-From-Harness-to-Weights.pdf)

## Research status

This is a systems research working paper, not a peer-reviewed publication
and not a deployment claim. It reports a bounded local experiment,
including failed runs, post-hoc diagnostics, matched controls, semantic
defects, and unresolved validation limits. Private case evidence and
model weights are not released.
